Three ways to close it

  • The X in the corner, or the "Never mind" button: both have data-bs-dismiss="modal".
  • Click the dark area outside the box.
  • Press Esc.

Bootstrap's JavaScript gives you all three for free.

The three pieces

  1. A button with data-bs-toggle="modal" and data-bs-target="#orderModal".
  2. A <div class="modal"> whose id matches that target, exactly.
  3. Bootstrap's script, at the end of the body.

Miss any one of the three and the button does nothing at all — no error message, no clue. That is the most common Bootstrap bug there is. (Example 00 is this page with the script deleted.)
