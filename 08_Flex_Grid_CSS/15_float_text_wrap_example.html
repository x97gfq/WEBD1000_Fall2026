Life on the Waterfront

Harbourside opened in 1987 in an old net loft at the end of Water Street. The first menu had three things on it: haddock, chips and tea. The boats tied up out back, and the fish went from the wharf to the fryer in about ten minutes.

Not much has changed. We still buy our haddock straight off the boats, cut our fries by hand every morning and make our own tartar sauce. The tea is still strong. What has changed is the view: the old fish plant is gone, and in its place is a boardwalk where people walk off their lunch.

"From the wharf to the fryer in ten minutes."

In the summer we open the back deck. Bring a sweater: the fog rolls in off the Gulf of Maine most afternoons, and it can drop ten degrees in a few minutes. Then the foghorn starts, and it feels like home.

Thanks for forty years. See you on the deck.
