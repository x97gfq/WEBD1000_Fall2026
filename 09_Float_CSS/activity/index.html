Fresh from the Wharf

We buy our haddock straight off the boats, cut our fries by hand, and make our tartar sauce every morning.

Harbourside opened in 1987 in an old net loft at the end of Water Street. In the summer we open the back deck, so bring a sweater: the fog rolls in off the Gulf of Maine most afternoons.
